The Kokkos team is announcing the first Kokkos Usergroup Meeting to be held in
Albuquerque New Mexico, USA April 23rd through 25th. The meeting will give the
growing Kokkos community a chance to present progress in adopting Kokkos,
exchange experiences, discuss challenges and help set priorities for the future
roadmap of Kokkos. Projects are invited to give a 20 minute presentation.
Application talks are encouraged to focus on technical and algorithmic
challenges in adopting Kokkos and how Kokkos’s capabilities were used to
overcome those.

Unfortunately we reached the capacity of our facility and can not accept more
registrations.
